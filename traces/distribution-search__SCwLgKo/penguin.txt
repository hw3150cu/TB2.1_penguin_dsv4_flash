## Approach

For vocabulary size `N = 150000` and uniform distribution `U_i = 1/N`, the two metrics reduce to simple moment constraints on the weight representation `w_i = N·p_i`:

- `D_KL(P||U) = mean(w·ln(w)) = 10`
- `D_KL(U||P) = -mean(ln(w)) = 10`

Because the desired forward KL is `10`, the distribution must be fairly concentrated (entropy ≈ 1.918 nats). I used a structured 3-level distribution:

- 1 token with high probability `A/N`
- 6 tokens with medium probability `B/N`
- the remaining 149,993 tokens with tiny probability `E/N`

`scipy.optimize.least_squares` solves the three nonlinear equations (sum = 1, forward KL = 10, backward KL = 10).
