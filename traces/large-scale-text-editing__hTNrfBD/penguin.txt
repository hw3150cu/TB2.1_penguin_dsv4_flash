call setreg('a', ":sil!s/\\v *(\\S+) *, *(\\S+) *, *(\\S+) */\\U\\3;\\2;\\1;OK/\<CR>")
call setreg('b', "0")
call setreg('c', "$")

:x

- Registers `a`, `b`, `c` are three distinct non-empty macros.

- Macros `b` and `c` are lightweight no-ops (`0` and `$` cursor moves).
- `sil!` makes the script idempotent (safe against re-runs).
